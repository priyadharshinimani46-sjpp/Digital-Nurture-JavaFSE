📝 Frequently Asked Questions
Go to the Register section on the portal. Fill in your full name, email address, preferred date, and select an event type. Click Register Now. A confirmation message will appear on the page. Make sure all fields are filled — the form will highlight any missing information.
The phone field requires exactly 10 digits (no spaces, dashes, or country code). The field is validated when you click away (on blur). If you see a red border, re-enter your number with exactly 10 digits and click outside the field.
  • 🎪 Summer Festival — FREE
  • 🏃 City Marathon — Rs. 200
  • 🎵 Open Air Concert — Rs. 150
  • 🎨 Art Exhibition — Rs. 100
  • 🍜 Food Carnival — Rs. 50
You can also check the fee in real time in the Feedback section by selecting an event from the dropdown.
This feature uses your browser’s Geolocation API. Make sure you:

1. Allow location access when the browser prompts you.
2. Use a supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).
3. If you previously denied permission, reset it: click the lock icon in the address bar → Site Settings → Location → Allow.
Your preferred event type is saved to your browser’s localStorage, so it persists even after you close the tab. A session copy is also stored in sessionStorage (clears when the tab is closed). Use Load Saved Preference to restore it, or Clear Preferences to remove both.
Ensure you have a stable internet connection, as the video loads from an external source. Try refreshing the page. Some corporate or school networks may block video streaming — in that case, try on a personal network.
The Dev Tools section is a learning exercise. Click the buttons to simulate console logs, warnings, and errors. Press F12 to open Chrome DevTools and see the real browser console alongside the on-page simulator. The Breakpoint Demo button uses the debugger statement — open the Sources tab to inspect variables when execution pauses.
In the Feedback section, double-click the festival image to enlarge it. Double-click again to return it to normal size.
If you have started filling in the registration form (entered your name), the browser will show a warning before you leave. This prevents accidental loss of your registration data. Complete or clear the form to leave without the warning.
